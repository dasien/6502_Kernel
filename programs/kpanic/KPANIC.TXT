                              K E R N E L   P A N I C


The conduit is corrupted. You are what is running down it.

Nothing in here is trying to kill you, exactly. The conduit simply costs you
energy to fly, and it does not stop, and when the energy is gone the kernel
panics. Everything else -- walls, daemons, firewalls -- is a way of spending
energy faster than you meant to.


CONTROLS

  left / right      steer
  SPACE             fire
  up / down         throttle -- slower is safer, faster covers more ground
  P                 pause
  Q                 quit

Throttle is not a difficulty setting. It writes the same number the world
speed reads, so it stays a decision you keep making: hold it back through a
narrow gauntlet, open it up over a clear stretch, and remember that distance
is half of what you are scored on.


THE ONE THING TO UNDERSTAND

There is a single ENERGY pool and it pays for everything.

  flying                 2 per row of conduit, always, no exceptions
  hitting the conduit    70
  flying into corruption 60
  taking a pellet        30

  a data node            +350, and it is the ONLY way to get energy back

You start with 1000. Below 250 the bar turns red.

Nothing else refills you. Not clearing a screen, not a firewall, not
surviving. Only nodes. They are deliberately scarce, and that scarcity is
the whole game: every node is a bet on how long you can keep going before
the next one.


NODES: THE CHOICE

A data node can be taken or shot. Never both.

  fly over it     +350 energy, no points
  shoot it        +25 points, and the energy is gone

Twenty-five points is not much. Three hundred and fifty energy is a
hundred and seventy-five rows of conduit. Shoot nodes when you are nearly
full and greedy; take them when you are not. Getting that wrong is how most
runs end.


WEAPONS

You begin with a plain shot straight up the column. Three others exist, each
with limited rounds, and each drops from wreckage:

  S   SPREAD    fires into adjacent columns too -- short-ranged, on purpose
  B   BEAM      a tall fast bolt that punches through what it hits
  H   HOMING    shots drift toward the nearest target

Picking one up while you already hold it deepens it instead, up to level 3.
When the rounds run out you fall back to the plain shot, which never runs dry.


FIREWALLS

Every 80 rows the conduit is barred by a firewall with a single port in it.

A firewall is a toll, not a door. Shoot the port open and pass through for 150
points, or take the hit and carry on -- 60 energy, and you keep moving. Which
one is right depends entirely on how much energy you have and whether you can
line up on the port in the room you have left.


SECTORS

  KERNEL -> HEAP -> STACK -> I/O -> KERNEL -> ...

The loop does not end. Sectors do not run faster -- your throttle is the only
thing that sets speed. What changes is density: more corruption and fewer
nodes as you go, planes withheld until the third sector, and the narrow
river only unlocking in the fifth. The geometry alternates with sector
parity, so the KERNEL you see the second time is not the one you learned.


SCORING

  data node shot     25        daemon     15
  firewall port      150       worm       25
                               sentinel   40

Distance is the other half, and it is the half that does not run out. The
question the game asks is how far you can go, so the score screen shows both.


ONE LAST THING

Read the drain, not the bar. The bar tells you where you are; the number at 25
tells you how many rows you have left, and rows are what you are spending.
