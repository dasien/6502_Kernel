                        T H E   S U N L E S S   V A U L T


For an age the Shimmering Orb hung above the Temple of Dawn, and its light was
the light of the world -- warmth in the fields, colour in the tide, the slow
sure turning of the seasons.

Then the Orb was taken.

A pale sorcerer, envious of the sun, hid it at the bottom of a vault fifteen
floors deep. The instant the Orb left the surface, the sky went out. Crops
greyed. The oceans stilled. Cold crept into every hearth, and the world has
waited, dimming, ever since.

Descend the Sunless Vault, floor by floor, past the things that have learned to
live without the sun, and take back the Orb from the guardian the sorcerer left
to keep it.

But know two things before you go:

  The Orb still remembers warmth, and every crawling, hungering thing in the
  vault is drawn to it. Once it is in your hands, more will come -- and keep
  coming -- the longer you hold it.

  And the sorcerer was no fool. He set a trap in the stone: the moment the Orb
  is lifted, the vault begins to seal itself. You will have only minutes to
  climb back into the light before the way grinds shut for good, and the dark
  keeps you -- and the Orb -- forever.

Recover the Orb. Climb out. Give the world back its warmth.


GETTING IN

The game lives in the SVAULT drawer. At the DOS ] prompt:

  OPEN SVAULT
  VAULT

Roll your hero (R to reroll the four stats, Enter to accept), type a name, and
you drop onto floor 1.


THE GOAL

Descend to floor 15, kill the Vault Guardian, and step onto the Shimmering Orb
to lift it. That opens a stairway and starts a real-time SEAL CLOCK -- climb
all the way back to the surface before it runs out. Reaching the top with the
Orb wins; dying or letting the vault seal loses.


CONTROLS

  arrow keys, or h j k l   move west / south / north / east
                           (bump a creature to attack it)
  SPACE or .               wait one turn
  >                        use the stairs you are standing on -- down, or UP
                           once you hold the Orb
  i                        character sheet: stats, HP/MP, XP, gold, weapon and
                           armour bonuses, carried potions and scrolls.
                           Press a letter to use one
  c                        cast a spell (lists the spells your INT allows)
  p                        pray at an altar (only while standing on one)
  Q                        quit to DOS

Opening a menu is free. USING an item or CASTING a spell costs a turn, and the
monsters act. One turn happens per key press.


WHAT YOU SEE

  @  yellow      you
  #  white       wall
  .  green       floor
  >  cyan        stairs
  omega, white   an altar -- stand on it and press p
  delta, yellow  the Shimmering Orb, on floor 15
  a letter       a creature; see the monster list below
  a shading      a shadow, the one creature not drawn as a letter
  an inverted !  a potion: yellow is healing, cyan is mana
  a triple bar   a scroll (magic map)
  a sunburst     a pile of gold
  gear shapes    weapon upgrade / armour upgrade
  dark grey      explored, but out of sight now -- you remember the layout,
                 not what has moved into it

You see only what your torch reaches. Rooms light up as you enter them;
corridors reveal as you go.


YOUR HERO

Four stats, rolled at the start (minimum 8) and raised permanently at altars:

  STR   melee damage
  INT   which spells you know, and the size of your mana pool
  CON   hit points
  DEX   accuracy and evasion

  HP    = 6 + CON + (level-1) * (4 + CON/6)
  Mana  = 2*INT + (level-1) * (INT/2)

Kills grant XP. Levelling up fully heals you and enlarges both pools; the
status bar shows progress as XP have/needed.

You regain about 1 HP every 15 turns and about 1 mana every 25 turns, so you
can rest by waiting on a safe tile. HP recovery pauses while you are poisoned.

Weapon and armour upgrades add a permanent +N to damage and to damage soaked.


COMBAT

When you bump a creature, or it attacks you:

  hit chance   70 + attacker accuracy - defender evasion, clamped to 5-95%
  damage       a roll in the attacker's range, minus the defender's armour,
               always at least 1 on a hit
  critical     10% chance, doubles the damage

Your accuracy rises with DEX and level, your damage with STR and your weapon
bonus, your armour with the armour bonus and the Shield spell.

The message log is two lines. The TOP line is what you do this turn; the BOTTOM
line is what the world does back. After a hit that does not kill, the top line
hints at what is left of the foe -- barely hurt, wounded, badly hurt, near
death -- so you can judge whether to press or back off.

  g   goblin       2-6     10

                               dark and hard to see; evasive

  z   zombie       3-8     20  slow but tough
  G   gnoll        4-9     18

  w   wraith       6-12    24  evasive, POISON

POISON stacks a few ticks per hit and drains HP each turn until it wears off,
shown as PSN on the status bar. Regenerators heal a little every turn they are
in view, so burst them down.

Monsters walk OVER gold and items without destroying them. Kill one standing on
loot and it drops where it falls.


PICKUPS

  gold                 currency. Spend it at altars; it also counts toward your
                       score. Picked up automatically, shown on the i page
  potion of healing    restores HP
  potion of mana       restores mana
  scroll of magic map  reveals a whole floor's layout
  weapon upgrade       permanent +1 to your attack
  armour upgrade       permanent +1 to your defence

Potions and scrolls go into your pack; use them from the i menu. Gold and gear
apply the instant you step on them.


SPELLS (press c)

You KNOW every spell your INT meets the requirement for. MANA limits how often
you cast. Casting costs a turn.

  spell           min INT  mana  effect
  Magic Missile        8      4  bolt the nearest creature you can see,
                                 3 + INT/2 + d4 damage
  Light                9      3  widens your torch for a while -- see farther,
                                 but it is not a full map
  Heal                10      8  restore 8 + INT HP
  Shield              11     14  +armour for several turns
  Teleport            12      2  blink to a random floor cell; a cheap escape
  Time Stop           15    all  freezes every monster AND the seal clock for
                                 about 5 + INT/4 turns. Needs at least 20 mana
                                 and consumes your entire pool

Raise INT at an altar to unlock more of the book.


ALTARS (press p)

Now and then a floor holds an altar. Stand on it and press p to spend gold:

  restore body and mind     refill HP and mana
  blessing of Might         permanent +1 STR
  blessing of Mind          permanent +1 INT, and a larger mana pool
  blessing of Vigor         permanent +1 CON, and more maximum HP
  blessing of Grace         permanent +1 DEX

Every purchase raises the price of the next. Your score counts the gold you
GATHER, not what is left in your purse, so spending at altars costs you nothing
on the scoreboard. Buy freely.


THE ESCAPE

Lifting the Orb changes everything.

A seal clock appears, SEAL mm:ss, and counts down in REAL TIME -- it ticks even
while you stand still and think. You have ten minutes. It turns red for the
last two.

The stairs now lead UP. Climb floor by floor back toward the surface. Each
floor is freshly generated and swarming, every room occupied: the Orb's warmth
draws everything to you.

Time Stop freezes the clock as well as the monsters, and opening a menu pauses
it. Use those windows wisely.

Reach the surface before 0:00 to win. If the clock runs out, the vault seals
you in.


SCORE

  depth, round trip (deepest reached + floors climbed back)    x10
  creatures slain                                               x3
  damage dealt                                                  /2
  healing done                                                  /4
  mana spent                                                    /4
  gold gathered (total found, not what is left)                 x1
  escaped with the Orb                                        +500

Getting deep and climbing back out is where the points are. A clean escape is
worth far more than any hoard.


ADVICE FROM THOSE WHO WENT DOWN BEFORE YOU

Rest between fights. Wait on a cleared tile to regain HP and mana before
pushing on.

Bank an escape. Keep enough mana for a Teleport -- it costs only 2 -- and save
Time Stop for a swarmed choke point during the climb.

Use the altars. Score counts gold gathered, not gold kept, so blessings are
effectively free on the scoreboard. Spend on whatever helps you survive.

Learn the depth bands. If you are seeing trolls and wyverns, the ogres and
gnolls are behind you; plan gear and spells for what is ahead.
