                          THE SUNLESS VAULT


For an age the Shimmering Orb hung above the Temple of Dawn, and its
light was the light of the world -- warmth in the fields, colour in the
tide, the slow sure turning of the seasons.

Then the Orb was taken.

A pale sorcerer, envious of the sun, hid it at the bottom of a vault fifteen floors deep.
The instant the Orb left the surface, the sky went out. Crops greyed. The oceans stilled.
Cold crept into every hearth, and the world has waited, dimming, ever since.

Descend the Sunless Vault, floor by floor, past the things that have
learned to live without the sun, and take back the Orb from the guardian
the sorcerer left to keep it.

But know two things before you go:

  The Orb still remembers warmth, and every crawling, hungering thing in
  the vault is drawn to it. Once it is in your hands, more will come --
  and keep coming -- the longer you hold it.

  And the sorcerer was no fool. He set a trap in the stone: the
  moment the Orb is lifted, the vault begins to seal itself. You will have
  only minutes to climb back into the light before the way grinds shut for
  good, and the dark keeps you -- and the Orb -- forever.

Recover the Orb. Climb out. Give the world back its warmth.
